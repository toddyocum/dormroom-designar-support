# Dormroom DesignAR — Privacy Policy

*Effective: August 2026*

Dormroom DesignAR ("the app") helps students scan, plan, and share dorm room
layouts. This policy explains what the app collects and how it's used.

## What the app collects

**Room scans.** The app uses your device's camera and LiDAR sensor to build a
floor plan of a room (walls, doors, windows, and furniture shapes). Camera
imagery is processed on your device by Apple's RoomPlan technology and is
never uploaded; only the resulting floor-plan geometry (measurements and
shapes) is stored.

**Content you choose to publish.** If you publish a room, its floor plan,
university, building, floor, room style, and optional room number are added
to the app's public database, where any user can view them. Notes you write
for future residents are also public. Publishing is always an explicit
action — nothing is shared unless you tap Publish or Post.

**Designs and registries.** Furniture layouts and registry claims you create
stay on your device (and in your personal iCloud, if enabled). They are not
visible to other users unless you share them yourself.

## What the app does not collect

- No account is created; the app uses your device's iCloud session.
- No name, email address, phone number, or contacts.
- No location tracking. Scans are not geotagged.
- No advertising identifiers and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
- No sale of data, to anyone, ever.

Published content is associated with an anonymous Apple-generated identifier
that other users never see. It exists so that content can be moderated and so
you can delete what you published.

## Where data lives

All shared data is stored in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) public database,
operated by Apple in the app's iCloud container. Your private designs sync
through your own iCloud account under Apple's privacy terms.

## Moderation, reporting, and deletion

- You can report any note; reported content is hidden on your device
  immediately and reviewed by the developer.
- You can block a publisher, which hides everything they've shared from your
  device.
- You can delete your own published rooms and notes at any time from within
  the app, which removes them from the public database.
- Retailer links open the retailer's website; their privacy policies apply
  there.

## Children

The app is intended for users 13 and older.

## Contact

Questions, moderation concerns, or deletion requests: please open an issue on

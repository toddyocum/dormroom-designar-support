# Dormroom DesignAR Support

## Getting Help

For support inquiries, bug reports, and feature requests, please use
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/toddyocum/dormroom-designar-support/issues).

When reporting a problem, it's helpful to include:

- Your iPhone model and iOS version
- What you were doing (scanning, designing, publishing, browsing)
- What you expected, and what happened instead

## Reporting Content or Moderation Concerns

Dormroom DesignAR includes community-shared floor plans and notes. To report
a problem with shared content:

- **In the app:** tap the ⋯ menu on any note to report it (hidden for you
  immediately and reviewed promptly) or to block that publisher entirely.
- **Urgent moderation concerns:** open a
  [GitHub Issue](https://github.com/toddyocum/dormroom-designar-support/issues)
  with the room's university, building, and floor. Reports are reviewed
  regularly and violating content is removed for all users.

You can delete anything you've published — rooms or notes — from within the
app at any time.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need a special iPhone to scan?**
Live room scanning uses the LiDAR sensor on iPhone 12 Pro or later (Pro
models). Any supported iPhone can browse rooms, design from templates, and
use registries.

**How accurate are the scans?**
Typically within an inch or two per wall. Every scan gets a review step
where you can check any wall against a tape measure and correct it with a
tap — mirrors and glass can occasionally confuse the sensor.

**Do I have to publish my scan?**
No. Scan a room, keep it private, and design away — private scans stay on
your device and never reach the community database. Publishing is always an
explicit choice you can make later, or never.

**Is my room number shared?**
Only if you choose to include it. You can publish a room as just
"building + floor" and it's still useful to future residents.

**Is it really free?**
Yes — free for students, no account, no tracking, and that won't change.
Product links open retailer websites and may earn the app a commission at no
cost to you; if a product placement is ever sponsored, it's labeled. Fit-check
results are never influenced by sponsorship.
